The market's own bar

On a corner of Santa Caterina Market since 1985. Joan and his team open at 7:30am for breakfast, and at lunchtime they cook home-style dishes with whatever arrives at the market stalls that morning.

There's a set lunch menu Monday to Friday; on Saturdays it's à la carte.
